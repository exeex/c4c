# BIR Route Index Retirement Ordered Follow-Up Plan

This handoff turns the research digest and ownership classification into an
ordered follow-up queue. It is not an implementation packet and does not create
new source ideas; Step 4 owns idea generation.

The priority model is dependency first, then blast-radius reduction: contract
the narrow public route facade with named aliases before moving consumers,
separate publication proof from prepared authority before stack work, and leave
test/dump cleanup until the named proof surfaces exist.

## Dependency Notes

1. Start with named aliases and proof adapters because they reduce the public
   route facade blast radius without changing behavior. Route builders may
   remain private compatibility while public callers move to ownership-named
   entry points.
2. Move consumers only one proof surface at a time. Route 4 prepared lookup
   attribution is the lowest-risk first consumer; Route 7 AArch64 comparison
   validation follows through a named comparison/control proof adapter.
3. Clean the publication boundary after the first facade consumers move. Route
   5 belongs later than Route 4 because it mixes real CFG-edge publication facts
   with stored `route5_status` and `route5_agrees` proof residue.
4. Keep stack/frame/value-home work separate from BIR facade retirement. BIR
   views may feed prepared producers, but only prepared records and prepared MIR
   views can authorize stack destinations or MIR lowering.
5. Retire test/dump route vocabulary only after the named proof surface exists.
   Dump spelling should reflect semantic migration; it must not lead it.

## Deferred Or Unassigned Work

- Route 1 producer, Route 2 select/control, Route 3 memory, Route 6 call, and
  Route 8 return-chain extraction are deferred until Step 4 creates focused
  follow-up ideas with one owning layer and one first migration surface each.
- Broad producer, memory, call, and return named-view extraction is unassigned
  in this handoff beyond the required Step 4 idea queue. The current umbrella
  does not choose implementation files or rewrite route builders.
- Route 5 facade-like cleanup is deferred until Route 4 and Route 7 facade
  consumers no longer require public route-index status and named publication
  proof exists.
- Ideas 647 and 655 are intentionally deferred. They should not be reactivated
  from route agreement, route facade status, dump rows, expectation changes, or
  allowlist evidence. They need a positive prepared producer seam above route
  dumps before any residual stack destination authority packet resumes.
