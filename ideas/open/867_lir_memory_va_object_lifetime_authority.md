## Goal

Publish one exact typed LIR producer/schema authority route for a remaining
memory, variadic-argument, object, or lifetime fact that is not already covered
by accepted local-object, VLA, or selected memcpy receiver history.

## Why This Exists

Idea 866 classified memory/VA and object/lifetime authority as missing a current
exact successor. 734 must not receive another memory or pointer row until LIR
first publishes structured current-function object, pointer, lifetime, or
variadic-argument authority that can be verified without display-text recovery.

## In Scope

- Select one bounded memory/VA/object/lifetime family with a concrete current
  LIR fact gap.
- Publish native LIR fields or schema carriers for that fact, plus producer
  population at the exact owned seam.
- Add verifier checks proving current-function ownership, validity, type or
  object/lifetime suitability, and malformed rejection for the selected fact.
- Write a handoff document naming the exact typed field set and the one future
  734 receiver row it authorizes.

## Out Of Scope

- Raw-BIR receiver implementation under 734.
- Reopening accepted local-object, VLA stack-save/restore, or selected memcpy
  rows.
- Broad pointer, memory, stack, lifetime, or VA sweeps.
- Recovering pointer, object, lifetime, or variadic state from operand spelling,
  printer output, LLVM text, rendered names, or testcase identity.
- Aggregate/vector, CFG/PHI, body-parameter, inline-assembly template, global
  policy, or terminal 797 convergence work.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Fresh build and focused producer/verifier proof cover the selected native
  memory/VA/object/lifetime fact and malformed rejection boundary.
- The handoff document identifies the exact LIR fields, owner checks, accepted
  positive case, rejected malformed cases, and one future 734 receiver row.
- Existing accepted local-object, VLA, and selected memcpy receipt tests remain
  valid and are not weakened or repurposed.
- No Raw-BIR receiver work lands in this idea.
